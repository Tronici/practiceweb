Some things about Loreli!


Lauren Graham (Lorelai Gilmore) plays the 32 year old, coffee loving Loreli Gilmore, who works at the Independance inn,

Lorelai Gilmore was born to Emily and Richard Gilmore in Connecticut. Her life growing up was complicated due to an overbearing mother and a workaholic father, who both expected Lorelai to live in their world of privilege. Lorelai is a highly talkative, free-spirited woman, who cares for here daughter Rory.


The famous eating habbits of Loreli Gilmore!

Lorelai's eating habits are famously unhealthy; she is a caffeine addict and subsists mostly on takeout,because whenever she cooks, it ends in disaster!Which means her daughter, Rory, sometimes gets into these habbits too. She and Rory have regular food-and-movie nights, during which they buy enough junk food for several people and devour most of it while watching a film. They very rarely invite people round to participate in this ritual.
