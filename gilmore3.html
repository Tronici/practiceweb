More info on Rory!


Rory is a 16yr old girl who lives in Stars Hollow and goes to Chilton private school

Rory Gilmore is the only daughter of Lorelai Gilmore the first born daughter of Christopher Hayden (her parents were 16 years old when Rory was born). She was born on October 8th, 1984 at 4:03 in the morning.Rory was raised in Stars Hollow where her mother originally worked at the Independence Inn.


Rory's family history!

Rory had nearly no contact with her grandparents, Richard and Emily Gilmore, when she was growing up, usually only seeing them during the holidays, because of her Mum's desicion to avoid connection with them. It wasn't until she began school at Chilton that her grandparents became a regular presence in her life, because of a deal Loreli made with them. Her father was rarely around during her childhood, because Loreli didn't want to marry him, so she grew up like her mother, and has an amazing connection with her.


Rory's working life

Lorelai had wanted Rory to attend Harvard since she was three years old, and Rory followed that dream too. At the start of the series, she is in high school, (Chilton)and when the series ends, she is a recent college graduate.
