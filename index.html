Some things about Gilmore Girls!


Gilmore Girls is an American comedy-drama television series created by Amy Sherman-Palladino and starring Lauren Graham (Lorelai Gilmore) and Alexis Bledel (Rory Gilmore).Gilmore Girls ran for seven seasons, ending its run on May 15, 2007. The series has two main characters: witty thirty-two year old single mother Lorelai Gilmore and her intellectual teenage daughter Rory Gilmore (short for Lorelai).
